## Story 1: AWS Fortune 500 Portfolio Turnaround
**Use for:** Enterprise program leadership · Budget management · Executive stakeholder mgmt

**Situation:** At AWS GenAI Innovation Center, three Fortune 500 customers had inconsistent cloud-spend trajectories — one was at risk of pausing further AI investment due to unclear ROI.

**Task:** Senior TPM across all three accounts with $1.4M combined program budget; convert exploratory engagements into production-scale GenAI programs.

**Action:**
- Built a unified KPI scoreboard tracking model deployments, business KPIs, and cloud-spend impact — published bi-weekly to VP/C-suite
- Reforecasted resource allocation to prioritize the at-risk account's most promising use case (claims automation)
- Stood up a weekly risk-review with engineering and vendor partners; instituted a dependency-blockers log
- Personally presented 15+ executive reviews tying technical milestones to business outcomes

**Result:** $28M+ in incremental cloud spend across portfolio. All three accounts in production. At-risk account expanded to multi-quarter follow-on engagement.

**60s version:** Led portfolio of 3 Fortune 500 AWS customers with $1.4M program budget. Built unified KPI dashboards, ran bi-weekly executive reviews, reforecasted resources. Delivered $28M+ cloud-spend impact and retained all three accounts.

## Story 2: Rakuten World-First 5G Release Governance
**Use for:** Risk management · Vendor/multi-team mgmt · Agile @ scale · Technical decision making

**Situation:** Rakuten Mobile launching world's first cloud-native 5G NSA network on Kubernetes — 9M+ subscribers — with CNFs/VNFs from 6+ vendors never integrated end-to-end.

**Task:** Owned release management and release governance for Symcloud Orchestrator (GitOps + CI/CD layer).

**Action:**
- Established risk register with 40+ cross-vendor dependencies; weekly risk-review with O-RAN/TIP partners
- Defined zero-touch provisioning standard; go/no-go gates per release
- Drove SAFe Agile across 4 ARTs and 15+ stakeholders including C-level execs
- Critical vendor missed a compliance check → escalated, swapped to parallel vendor track, kept launch on critical path

**Result:** Deployment time dropped 37%, network launched on schedule, platform supported 110+ enterprise clients in year one. Release-governance playbook became Rakuten's standard.

**60s version:** Owned release governance for world's first cloud-native 5G on K8s. 40+ multi-vendor dependencies, SAFe across 4 ARTs, weekly risk reviews. Dropped deployment time 37%, launched on time, 110+ enterprise clients.

## Story 6: Conflict Resolution — Engineering vs Product at Rakuten
**Use for:** Conflict resolution · Influencing without authority · Communication

**Situation:** Engineering lead and product VP disagreed on whether to ship a feature with a known performance edge case or delay 6 weeks to fix it.

**Task:** As Program Manager, owned the release decision; needed to align both leaders without authority over either.

**Action:**
- Quantified risk + business cost of both paths — built one-page decision brief
- 1:1 with each leader first to surface real concerns
- Convened 30-minute decision meeting; proposed phased release (ship to 5% traffic, monitor, expand)
- Got signoff from both within the meeting

**Result:** Phased release deployed on original date. Edge case never materialized at scale. Engineer + VP later co-presented the model as the team's release-decision playbook.

**60s version:** Engineer wanted to delay; VP wanted to ship. Built one-page risk-vs-cost brief, did 1:1s, proposed phased release. Both agreed in 30 min, shipped on time, no incidents.

## Story 7: Psyk AI Scrum Master Pilot — Quick-Cycle Learning
**Use for:** Scrum Master experience · Agile facilitation · Stakeholder alignment

**Situation:** Psyk AI running 6-month pilot with U-M Health to train psychology students using AI agents — clinical and product priorities frequently diverged.

**Task:** Scrum Master facilitating 6-month delivery while keeping clinical + product roadmaps aligned.

**Action:**
- Ran 12 sprints with disciplined planning/demo/retro cadence
- Used OKRs to align clinical outcomes with product KPIs at each sprint review
- Brought clinical leads into sprint reviews (rare for clinical pilots) — built trust
- Integrated RAG pipeline (Ragas framework) iteratively rather than big bang

**Result:** Pilot hit 92% stakeholder alignment (post-pilot survey). Improved answer relevancy 27%. Won industry award.

**60s version:** Scrum Master on 6-month clinical AI pilot with U-M Health. 12 sprints, OKR-driven, clinical leads in reviews. 92% stakeholder alignment, 27% relevancy improvement, award-winning.
