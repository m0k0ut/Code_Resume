## TMAY Script (90 seconds)

> "I've spent 12+ years in enterprise IT — the last 7 specifically in program management. The shortest version: I take complex technical programs from messy starting state to predictable delivery.
>
> Most recently, I was Senior Technical Program Manager at AWS's GenAI Innovation Center, owning a portfolio of 3 Fortune 500 customers with a $1.4M program budget. We delivered $28M+ in business impact through cloud migration and GenAI adoption, with bi-weekly KPI reporting to C-suite.
>
> Before AWS, I ran the cloud platform program at Rakuten Mobile that delivered the world's first cloud-native 5G network on Kubernetes — 9M+ subscribers, multi-vendor CNF/VNF release governance on SAFe Agile. And at Servify, I scaled the Samsung Care+ B2B program from 2 to 13 countries across 9 partners.
>
> I'm PMP and PMI-ACP certified, with a STEM-designated MBA from U-M Ross. I'm based on the West Coast right now but moving to Michigan — Ann Arbor is where I trained, and the 4-day hybrid is genuinely a feature for me, not a friction.
>
> The reason this role caught my eye: the JD reads like a checklist of what I've been doing — enterprise programs, PMO governance, executive reporting, vendor coordination. I'd like to bring that to Miracle."

## 30/60/90 Day Plan

**Days 1-30 — Learn:**
- Map all active programs, their stakeholders, current health
- Sit in on standups, steering committees, executive reviews
- Understand Miracle's PMO standards, templates, tooling stack
- 1:1s with engineering leads, account leads, executive sponsors

**Days 31-60 — Stabilize:**
- Identify 2-3 highest-risk programs; introduce risk register + weekly review cadence
- Standardize executive reporting template across portfolio
- Establish my own cadence with key clients

**Days 61-90 — Optimize:**
- Propose 1-2 PMO process improvements (dependency tracking, vendor scorecards)
- Lead my first quarterly business review with leadership
- Begin coaching/mentoring junior PMs
